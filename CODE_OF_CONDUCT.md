# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully regardless of background, identity, experience,
or viewpoint. Critique code and engineering decisions with concrete evidence;
do not attack people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized
attention, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to project discussions, reviews, and project events.
When moderation is available, maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict
participation in proportion to the behavior and its impact. Apply decisions
fairly and respect the privacy of affected people.

This repository is in initial development and has no published moderation
contact yet. Establish a private reporting channel before inviting a wider
community. Do not post sensitive incident details in public discussions.
